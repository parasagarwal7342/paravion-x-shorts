## Monetization targets (YouTube Partner Program)
- 1,000 subscribers **and** 4,000 public watch hours from long videos in the last 12 months, **or** 10M public Shorts views in 90 days.
- Watch-hour math: 4,000 h ÷ 8-min videos ≈ 30,000 full views. At 2 long videos/day, that's ~500 full views per video over a month.

## The PARAVION X format (every long video)
- **Promise:** "The world's biggest trending story, explained and fact-checked, in ~8–10 minutes."
- **Title formula:** `[Big fact/number] + [Topic]: [What We Know / Explained]`, e.g. "GTA VI Made $3.79 BILLION in One Day: The Full Story, Explained".
- **Structure:** hook (0:00–0:30) → what happened → the background → what's disputed → reactions → **Fake or Fact?** (signature segment) → what happens next → one-word comment prompt.
- **Thumbnail:** one huge number or 2–3 words + one real photo + PARAVION X logo, same layout every time.
- **Schedule:** long video daily 7 PM IST; Short 1 PM pointing to it.
- **Playlists:** "Tech & Money, Explained", "World News, Explained", "Gaming & Entertainment, Explained", "Fake or Fact?".
- **Daily topic pick:** the most-searched story of the day worldwide (Google Trends, YouTube trending, X trending), confirmed by 2+ major outlets.
