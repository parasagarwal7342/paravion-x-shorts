# PARAVION X — zero-budget growth plan

Goal: grow YouTube, X and Instagram without paid ads. Everything here is free.

## The agents (automatic)

| Agent | When (IST) | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **1. News Producer** | Daily 6:23 AM | Finds the day's top story, fact-checks it against 2+ sources, writes, voices and renders a Short, and pushes it to GitHub. |
| **2. Publisher** | Daily 8:47 AM | Re-checks today's news facts, then schedules the new Short on YouTube + X at 1 PM. Tops up the Buffer queues with the next "Fake or Fact?" Shorts. Adds a text-only X post (quick fact, quiz or poll) when a slot is free. Drafts 5 reply ideas for you to post by hand. |
| **3. Growth Analyst** | Sundays 11:13 AM | Pulls the week's numbers from Buffer, ranks hooks and topics by performance, and changes next week's topic mix. Reports what worked, what didn't, and one experiment to try. |

The agents never auto-reply, auto-follow or auto-like. That breaks X and YouTube rules and can get the accounts limited. Replies are drafted for you to post yourself.

## Free growth levers, in order of impact

1. **Speed on news.** Shorts on a story within hours of it trending get pushed hardest. The 6:23 AM producer plus the 1 PM slot is built for this.
2. **The first 2 seconds.** Every hook is a number, a myth or a question. The analyst tracks average % viewed per hook type, and the next scripts copy the winners.
3. **Consistency.** 2 Shorts every day at the same times (1 PM and 7:30 PM IST). The algorithm and viewers both reward a schedule.
4. **Searchable titles.** Put the keyword first ("Nvidia CEO…", "UPI…", "CIBIL…"). Evergreen explainers keep getting found through search for months.
5. **Series and playlists.** Make two playlists in YouTube Studio: "Fake or Fact?" and "News in 60 Seconds". Add each Short as it goes live, so binge-watching keeps people on the channel.
6. **Pinned comment with a question** on every Short (it's in each PACKAGE.md). Comments are a strong ranking signal. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
7. **Cross-posting.** The same video goes native on YouTube Shorts, X and Instagram Reels. Facebook Reels is free too, and Buffer can post there if you connect a Facebook Page.
8. **15 minutes a day of replies on X**, by hand. Reply with a useful fact to big news accounts and creators in your niche within minutes of their posts. The Publisher drafts 5 replies a day. This is the fastest free way to get new followers on X.
9. **YouTube Community posts.** Polls and quizzes (see brand/social/POSTS.md) keep subscribers engaged between uploads.
10. **Collaborations.** Swap shout-outs with other small explainer or news channels (1k–10k subscribers) through comments or DMs. Free and effective.

## Weekly scorecard (the Growth Analyst fills this)
- Posts published, views, average % viewed, likes, comments, shares, new subscribers/followers
- Top 3 and bottom 3 Shorts, with their hook types
- Topic mix next week (news vs science vs money vs world)
- One experiment, e.g. a question hook vs a number hook, or a 30 s vs 50 s length

## Free-plan limits to know
- Buffer free: 3 channels, 10 queued posts per channel, YouTube max 2 posts a day. The Publisher tops up daily.
- ElevenLabs free: 10,000 characters a month (about 14 Shorts). Others use the preview voice until a new key is added.
