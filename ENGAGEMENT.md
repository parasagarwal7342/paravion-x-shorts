# PARAVION X: approval-first engagement desk

**Rule #1: nothing goes out without the owner's "yes".** Every reply, comment and DM is a draft. Only scheduled posts (Shorts and text posts) are published automatically, through Buffer, after approval.
**Rule #2: real accounts only.** No fake accounts, bots, bought likes/views, auto-follow, auto-like or follow-unfollow.
**Rule #3: facts stay safe.** Every factual claim in a reply needs 2+ reliable sources, or it isn't written. No financial, medical or legal advice. On contested topics, say what's confirmed and what isn't. Never put words in a real person's mouth.

## The four roles (all run inside the daily Publisher at 8:47 AM IST)

| Role | Covers | What it drafts each day | How it goes out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comment Replier** | Comments on our YouTube Shorts (read with a free YouTube Data API key). | **X Content** | Our own X timeline. | 1–2 text posts a day (a quick fact, a "Fake or Fact?" quiz, a "this day in…" note) plus a weekly thread recapping the week's Shorts. Keeps the pinned post fresh. | After approval, scheduled through Buffer (free plan: 10 queued per channel). |
| **Daily Report** | Everything above. | Comments handled, questions that keep coming up (these become new Short ideas), and which posts and hooks reached the most people. | Delivered in this session each morning. |

## How approval works
1. The morning report lists drafts with numbers: `R1–R5` replies, `D1–D5` DMs, `P1–P2` posts, `C1…` comment replies.
2. Reply with what you approve, e.g. **"approve P1 P2 C1-C4, skip R3"**, or edit one: **"P1 but say 'about' instead of 'over'"**.
3. Approved posts get scheduled. Approved replies and DMs come back as ready-to-paste text, plus a link to the post when known.

## What is not automated, and why
- **Instagram comments:** Buffer can't read them, and the Instagram API needs an approved Meta app. Paste or screenshot comments and they get replies drafted in seconds.
- **X replies and DMs:** reading and replying on X needs X's paid API, and automating replies breaks X's rules. They're drafted, and you post them.
