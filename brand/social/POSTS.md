"The sky is blue because it reflects the ocean." Fake or fact? 🤔

❌ FAKE.
Air molecules scatter blue sunlight far more than red (Rayleigh scattering). At sunset, light crosses more air, so the blue scatters away and reds remain.

### IG 3 · Fake or Fact: GPS · Mon Nov 24, 6:00 PM · `ig_03_fof_gps.png`
```
"Your phone's GPS needs the internet to work." Fake or fact? 📡

❌ FAKE.
GPS is receive-only: your phone listens to satellite signals and sends nothing back. Four satellites fix your position. Only your maps app may use data.

```
"Checking your own CIBIL score lowers it." Fake or fact? 💳

❌ FAKE.
Checking your own score is a soft enquiry and doesn't lower it. Hard enquiries happen when lenders check you for new credit, and many in a short time can slightly dent your score.
