# PARAVION MED: zero-budget growth plan

## Why this niche works
- Medical students rewatch and save short, exam-shaped content: "Beck's triad in 60 seconds" is searched every exam season.
- News moves fast (FDA/CDSCO approvals, NEJM/Lancet trials, NEET-PG dates and cut-offs), and few channels explain it
  accurately in under a minute. Being first **and** correct on exam news is the biggest lever.
- Evergreen pearls keep pulling search traffic for years; news brings the spikes.

## Levers, in order of impact
1. **Exam calendar speed.** NBEMS / NMC / USMLE announcements get a Short the same day (official notice only).
2. **The first 2 seconds.** A direct exam question ("Can you name Beck's triad?"), a surprising number, or a myth.
3. **Playlists per specialty and per series** (Cardiology, Neuro, Pharm, Emergency, Myths), so one Short leads to ten.
4. **Pinned question on every Short** and a daily Community-tab MCQ. Comments from students asking for topics feed the
   Evergreen Writer.
5. **Consistency:** 1 PM news, 7:30 PM evergreen, every day.
6. **Keyword-first titles:** "Horner Syndrome in 60 Seconds (PMA)", "qSOFA vs SIRS: what the 2021 guidelines say".
7. **Cross-post natively** to Instagram Reels (huge med-student community) and X.
8. **Collaborate** with small med-ed creators (1k–20k subs): swap Shorts ideas, duets, shout-outs. Never pay for engagement.
9. **Exam-season series:** in the 6 weeks before NEET-PG, a "last-minute high-yield" daily series.
10. **Trust signals:** sources in every description, the disclaimer, corrections pinned when we get something wrong.

## YouTube health-content rules to respect
- YouTube removes medical misinformation that contradicts local health authorities or WHO. Our sourcing rules keep us
  on the right side; never "both-sides" settled science.
- No graphic surgical or patient imagery (we only use drawn graphics, so this stays easy).
- Label synthetic/AI media only if realistic people or events are generated (we don't).

## Weekly scorecard
Posts, views, average % viewed, % viewed at 3 s, likes, comments, shares, saves, new subscribers; top/bottom 3 with hook
type and specialty; next week's mix; one experiment.
