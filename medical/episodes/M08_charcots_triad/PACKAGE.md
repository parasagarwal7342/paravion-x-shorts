## Summary
Acute cholangitis: Charcot's triad is fever, right upper quadrant pain and jaundice. Adding hypotension and confusion gives Reynolds' pentad, which signals severe disease. The triad is specific but has low sensitivity, so the Tokyo Guidelines 2018 use broader clinical, lab and imaging criteria.
