## Summary
A 1995 JAMA meta-analysis of 23 blinded studies found sugar did not affect children's behaviour or cognition (a small effect in some children can't be ruled out). In one study, mothers told their child had sugar rated them as more hyperactive even when it was a placebo. Sugar still matters for teeth and weight.

**Verdict:** Sugar causes hyperactivity: FALSE. Expectation changes perception: True.
