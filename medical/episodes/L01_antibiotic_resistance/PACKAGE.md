## Description
```
Drug-resistant bacteria directly caused 1.14 million deaths in 2021 and were linked to about 4.7 million. This deep dive covers how penicillin changed medicine, the four ways bacteria resist antibiotics, what WHO's 2025 surveillance found (1 in 6 infections resistant worldwide, 1 in 3 in WHO's South-East Asia region), and what actually works: using antibiotics only when needed, preventing infections, vaccines and stewardship.
```
