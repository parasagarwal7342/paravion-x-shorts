## Summary
The Glasgow Coma Scale scores eyes (1-4), verbal (1-5) and motor (1-6), for a total of 3 to 15. The lowest score is 3, not 0. In head injury, a GCS of 8 or less is a classic sign the airway needs protecting (NICE NG232).
