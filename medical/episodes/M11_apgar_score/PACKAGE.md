## Summary
The Apgar score rates 5 signs (appearance, pulse, grimace, activity, respiration) from 0 to 2 each, at 1 and 5 minutes after birth. Resuscitation should start before the 1-minute score if needed, and a low score alone doesn't prove asphyxia (AAP/ACOG).
