## Summary
A Cochrane review of 29 trials (11,306 people) found regular vitamin C did not reduce how often most people catch colds. It shortened colds slightly (about 8% in adults, 14% in children). Taking it after symptoms start showed no consistent effect. People under extreme physical stress were the exception.

**Verdict:** Vitamin C prevents colds: FALSE. Slightly shorter colds: True.
