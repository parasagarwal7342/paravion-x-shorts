## Summary
On Nov 9, 2026 the FDA approved asundexian (Sunharbo), the first factor XIa inhibitor, with antiplatelet therapy for adults after a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke or high-risk TIA. In the OCEANIC-STROKE trial it cut ischemic stroke by 26% (relative) with similar major bleeding (1.9% vs 1.7%), but GI bleeding was more common. It is not approved for atrial fibrillation.

**Verdict:** Confirmed: fewer repeat ischemic strokes vs placebo, similar major bleeding. Still open: long-term, real-world performance.
