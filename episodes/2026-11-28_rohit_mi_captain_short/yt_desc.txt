Rohit Sharma back as Mumbai Indians captain for IPL 2027? Real or fake, explained in 60 seconds.

It's REAL. Mumbai Indians confirmed on 27 Nov 2026 that Rohit Sharma will captain them in IPL 2027, three years after Hardik Pandya replaced him. Hardik was traded to Punjab Kings for Arshdeep Singh this month (₹18 crore each), after MI finished last in 2024, lost Qualifier 2 in 2025 and came ninth in 2026. Rohit captained MI in five IPL finals and won all five. The 5:45 PM announcement time (5 titles + jersey No. 45) is a fan theory; MI hasn't confirmed it.
