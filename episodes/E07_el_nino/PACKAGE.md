## Summary
El Niño is a warming of the tropical Pacific every 2–7 years. It is often linked to weaker Indian monsoons, but not always: in 1997, despite a very strong El Niño, India's monsoon was about 102% of normal.

**Verdict:** El Niño always means drought: FALSE. It raises the risk: True.
