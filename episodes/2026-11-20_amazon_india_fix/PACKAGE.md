## Summary
Amazon plans to invest $7 billion in Indian renewable energy by 2030, on top of $4 billion already contracted. Four newly announced projects totaling 934 MW in Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan are part of that existing $4 billion portfolio, which will total more than 2 GW once operational. Amazon has not said which projects the additional $7 billion will fund.

**Verdict:** The $7B pledge is confirmed. The 934 MW projects belong to the existing $4B. What the $7B will fund is not disclosed.
