## Summary
In India, CIBIL scores run from 300 to 900. Checking your own score is a soft enquiry and doesn't lower it. Hard enquiries come from lenders when you apply for credit; many in a short time can slightly dent it.

**Verdict:** Checking your score lowers it: FALSE. Self-checks are soft enquiries: True.
