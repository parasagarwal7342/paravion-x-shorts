## Summary
GPS is receive-only: satellites broadcast time and position, and your phone listens. Four satellites fix your position. Satellite clocks gain about 38 microseconds a day from relativity and are corrected.

**Verdict:** GPS needs mobile data: FALSE. GPS is free satellite radio: True.
