## Summary
Deepfakes are AI-made fake videos or audio. Some show glitches like odd blinking or lip sync, but better tools hide them. The safest check: who posted it, and are trusted outlets reporting it?

**Verdict:** You can always spot one by eye: FALSE. Check the source: True.
