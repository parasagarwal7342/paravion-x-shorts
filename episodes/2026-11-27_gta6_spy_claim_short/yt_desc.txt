Is GTA 6 spying on you? The viral insurer claim, fact-checked in 60 seconds.

A viral X post says a fired Rockstar engineer found a hidden GTA 6 system, "Lens", that scores players' impulse control and aggression and sells the profiles to insurers through a Take-Two company called "Vantage Analytics". Fact-checkers found no evidence: that company isn't in any of Take-Two's SEC filings, the account is anonymous and was created in August, and the "firing email" shows signs of a fake. The twist: Take-Two's real privacy policy does build "profile inferences" and shares them with advertisers (not insurers), and in many places you can opt out.

Verdict: FAKE (no evidence).
