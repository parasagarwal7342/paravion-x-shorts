## Summary
Black Friday is Nov 27, 2026. Adobe expects electronics discounts to peak at 30% off (deepest electronics and computer cuts on Cyber Monday). In India in 2025, DataWeave found electronics were already ~23% off before Black Friday week, which added under 1%. In the US, Target matches its own lower prices until Dec 24 and Best Buy until Jan 15 for most items. US credit card liability for unauthorized charges is capped at $50, and the FTC says anyone demanding gift cards, wire transfers or crypto is a scammer.

**Verdict:** Black Friday deals: real. Without a plan: overhyped.
