## Summary
The Rule of 72: divide 72 by the yearly rate to estimate years to double. At 8%, money doubles in about 9 years. It works against you too: at 6% inflation, prices double in about 12 years. It's an estimate.

**Verdict:** The Rule of 72 is exact: FALSE. A close estimate: True.
