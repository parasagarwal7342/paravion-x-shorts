## Summary
The Moon's gravity raises two ocean bulges, giving most coasts two high tides a day. The Sun's tidal pull is about half the Moon's; when they line up at new and full moon we get spring tides.

**Verdict:** Only the Moon makes tides: FALSE. The Sun adds about half: True.
