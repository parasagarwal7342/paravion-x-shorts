## Summary
The sky isn't blue because it reflects the ocean. Air molecules scatter blue sunlight far more than red (Rayleigh scattering). At sunset, light crosses more air, leaving reds and oranges.

**Verdict:** Sky reflects the ocean: FALSE. Air scatters blue light: True.
