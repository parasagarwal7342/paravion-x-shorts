## Summary
Earth's orbit takes about 365.2422 days, so a leap day every four years slightly overshoots. Century years skip it unless divisible by 400: 1900 wasn't a leap year, 2000 was, 2100 won't be.

**Verdict:** Leap year every 4 years, always: FALSE. Most century years skip it: True.
